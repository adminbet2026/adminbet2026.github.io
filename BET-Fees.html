Questions

The honest answers

Do you take insurance?
Yes, two ways. I'm in-network with select plans through Headway; booking through my Headway page shows your exact copay in about a minute. For all other plans I'm out-of-network, and most PPO plans reimburse 40 to 70% of the fee after your deductible is met. With Thrizer, you can pay just your estimated share at each session while it handles the claims.
What exactly is a superbill?
An itemized receipt for therapy. It lists your session dates, the service and diagnosis codes, and my provider number, which is everything your insurance company needs to process an out-of-network claim. You submit it to your insurer (most have an online portal), and they reimburse you directly.
How much will I actually get back?
It depends on your plan's out-of-network benefits and deductible, but 40 to 70% of the session fee is common once the deductible is met. I can help you find out before your first session, so there are no surprises.
Can I use my HSA or FSA?
Yes. Therapy is a qualified medical expense, and you can pay with an HSA or FSA card at every session. Many clients combine this with the superbill reimbursement.
What if I need to cancel?
Life happens, and I ask for 24 hours' notice when it does. The full policy is here: BET cancellation policy.
Will I know my costs up front?
Always. Under the federal No Surprises Act, you're entitled to a Good Faith Estimate of your expected costs, and I provide one at the start of care and any time fees change. No surprise bills, ever.
